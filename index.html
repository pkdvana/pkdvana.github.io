Vana Phalguna Krishna Das

Ultrafast, terahertz, and nonlinear optical spectroscopy

BS–MS graduate in Physical Sciences from IISER Kolkata. My research interests include ultrafast spectroscopy of condensed-matter and functional molecular systems, with an emphasis on terahertz time-domain spectroscopy, femtosecond pump–probe measurements, nonlinear optics, experimental development, and first-principles calculations.

Research Focus

  • Terahertz Time-Domain Spectroscopy
  • Femtosecond Pump–Probe Spectroscopy
  • Surface Second-Harmonic Generation
  • Ultrafast Electronic and Lattice Dynamics
  • Experimental Automation and Data Acquisition
  • First-Principles Vibrational Calculations

I am also interested in extending this work toward attosecond science, high-harmonic generation, and time-resolved ARPES.
