Ultrafast & Terahertz Spectroscopy Laboratory — IISER Kolkata

Master’s research under Prof. N. Kamaraju · December 2023 – Present

Terahertz Time-Domain Spectroscopy

I used terahertz time-domain spectroscopy to investigate polymer composites and microplastic samples across the 0.4–2.5 THz range. From transmitted THz waveforms, I retrieved the frequency-dependent refractive index, extinction coefficient, absorption, and complex conductivity. For PVDF–PANI composites, I examined how PANI loading modifies the dielectric response and THz shielding behaviour, and compared Maxwell–Garnett and Bruggeman effective-medium descriptions across composition. This work is being developed into a manuscript on PVDF–PANI composites for THz shielding.

Femtosecond Pump–Probe Spectroscopy

I carried out femtosecond transient-reflectivity measurements on aluminium thin films to probe their ultrafast optical response following photoexcitation. Using degenerate and nondegenerate pump–probe configurations, including a 400 nm pump and 800 nm probe, I measured thickness-dependent ΔR/R dynamics in films ranging from approximately 10 to 60 nm. The measurements revealed coherent Brillouin oscillations associated with propagating acoustic phonons, providing a route to study the interplay between ultrafast electronic excitation, lattice response, and strain propagation in confined metallic films.

I also contributed to the alignment, experimental setup, and data acquisition for pump–probe measurements on 1T-TaS2, gaining experience with ultrafast studies of correlated layered materials.

Experimental Development, Automation, and Nonlinear Optics

I built, aligned, and optimized ultrafast optical experiments including THz time-domain spectroscopy, femtosecond pump–probe, and surface second-harmonic generation measurements. This involved beam routing, focusing, polarization control, temporal and spatial overlap, lock-in detection, cryogenic measurements, and systematic signal optimization. I also developed a shaker-based fast-scan THz-TDS workflow to reduce acquisition time while retaining reliable waveform reconstruction.

For surface SHG measurements on Te-doped GaAs, I independently aligned the optical setup and developed a Python-based data-acquisition program for automated measurements. I also wrote custom tools for instrument control, live monitoring, logging, and measurement automation, including interfaces for the SR830 lock-in amplifier, ESP301 motion controller, and Cryo-Con 32B temperature controller.

Vibrational Analysis and First-Principles Calculations

I combined THz time-domain spectroscopy with first-principles vibrational calculations to investigate the low-frequency modes of a benzoxazolium–coumarin donor–π–acceptor chromophore. Using Gaussian16 calculations at the B3LYP-D3/6-311++G(d,p) level, I compared calculated vibrational frequencies and mode patterns with the measured THz spectrum to assign the observed features. This work connects molecular structure and intermolecular motion to the material’s low-frequency optical response and formed part of a collaborative study currently under revision. The preprint is available on arXiv .

Quantum Theory of Functional Materials Laboratory — IISER Kolkata

Independent Study under Dr. Bheemalingam Chittari · August – December 2024

  • Developed Quantum ESPRESSO workflows for bilayer graphene and graphene–TMD heterostructures, from structural relaxation through band structure, total and projected density of states, and k-resolved electronic-structure calculations.
